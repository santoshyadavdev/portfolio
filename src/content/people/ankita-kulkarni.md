resources to help developers build production-ready applications with React
and Next.js. Her Modern Full Stack Next.js course uses hands-on exercises,
interactive demos, and projects to teach the framework from fundamentals
through advanced patterns. She also shares practical development tips through
her Frontend Snacks newsletter.
